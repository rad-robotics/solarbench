## Why
The maintainers work in the field the benchmark measures. A benchmark has to say how any entry of its maintainers' own is made; undisclosed access has damaged trust in benchmarks before ([Epoch AI on FrontierMath](https://epoch.ai/blog/openai-and-frontiermath)).

## What
- State that maintainers' entries are produced by the same command from the public tree, are labelled as theirs in every table, and use no episode, asset or setting that other entrants cannot use.
- State who may see held-out material once a hidden split exists (`open-hidden-split`).
